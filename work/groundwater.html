What is Groundwater?

Client
NSW State Government - Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water
Services
  • Art direction
  • Concept development
  • Visual storytelling
  • Design and storyboards
  • Illustration
  • Animation

Groundwater is easy to manage badly and hard to explain well, because nobody can see it. After prolonged drought put the state's groundwater systems under public scrutiny, the Department needed to help people understand how groundwater works and how access and management decisions get made.

I led creative direction, illustration and motion design on an explainer built around a calm, clear visual narrative. It translates technical and regulatory content into something the public can follow without a background in hydrology. Seasonal cycles became the visual device for showing time passing and groundwater recovering.

The animation launched on the Department's website and social channels in long and short cuts. Its reception led to a six-part animation series, extending the same approach across the core aspects of groundwater management.
