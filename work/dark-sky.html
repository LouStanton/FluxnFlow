Dark Sky

An animated astronomer shows school children why dark night skies are worth protecting.

Client
NSW State Government - Department of Planning and Environment
Services
  • Art direction
  • Storyboards
  • Design & illustration
  • Motion capture
  • Animation
  • Music and sound effects (Oscar Urrutia Blanco)
Production
Outshine Media

The challenge

The NSW Department of Planning and Environment wanted school-aged children to understand why night skies need protecting from light pollution: a complex scientific message for a young audience.

The approach

I led the creative development, working with astronomer, writer and science communicator Fred Watson. His animated alter ego was brought to life in Adobe Character Animator, using in-camera motion capture and real-time lip-syncing to create a guide children could relate to. Storytelling, character design and sound were built to hold a young audience's attention.

How it was used

The animation formed part of the Department's Dark Sky Stage 3 education unit. It shows how light pollution affects astronomy, wildlife, ecosystems and human health, in a way that supports curriculum outcomes.
