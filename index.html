What I do

From information to understanding.

Find the story

Before anything is designed, I work out who needs to understand, what is in their way, and the story that gets past it. You get a creative direction your team and your experts can sign off.

Strategy • Concept development • Art direction

Make it understood

When a subject is invisible, technical or contested, animation lets people see it. Explainers, campaign films and social cut-downs, checked with your experts so nothing true gets lost.

Animation • Motion graphics • Storyboards • Illustration

Make it recognisable

Identity and campaign design that keep one story consistent, from the consultation document to the social tile.

Brand identity • Campaign design • Infographics

Make it readable

Long documents, designed so people can find what affects them and understand it. Clear structure, charts that explain, and a summary people will read.

Long Term Plans • Annual Plans • Annual Reports • Consultation documents
