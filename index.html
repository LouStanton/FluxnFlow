What I do

From information to understanding.

Make it understood

When a subject is invisible, technical or contested, animation lets people see it. Explainers, campaign films and social cut-downs, checked with your experts so nothing true gets lost.

Animation • Motion graphics • Storyboards • Illustration

Find the story

Before anything is designed, I work out who needs to understand, what is in their way, and the story that gets past it. You get a creative direction your team and your experts can sign off.

Strategy • Concept development • Art direction

Make it recognisable

Identity and campaign design that keep one story consistent, from the consultation document to the social tile.

Brand identity • Campaign design • Infographics

Why work with me

One senior person, start to finish.

The person you brief is the person who makes the work. No account managers, no handover to juniors.

I have run my own studio since 2011, and most recently worked inside a council communications team. I know sign-off chains, plain language duties, accessibility standards and consultation deadlines. You will not need to explain how government works.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Talk

    Tell me the subject, the audience and the deadline. I will say plainly whether I am the right fit.

  2. Plan

    You get a fixed scope and price, with expert review rounds built in.

  3. Make

    You see storyboards before animation starts, so changes are cheap and nothing arrives as a surprise.

  4. Deliver

    One main piece, plus the cut-downs, stills, captions and files your channels need.
